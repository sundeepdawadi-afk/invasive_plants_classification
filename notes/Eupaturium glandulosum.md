# Eupatorium glandulosum Kunth

> **Current Accepted Botanical Name:** [*Ageratina adenophora* (Spreng.) R.M.King & H.Rob.](<26 species/Ageratina adenophora.md>)  
> **Nepali Name:** कालो वनमारा (Kaalo Banmara)  
> **Family:** [Asteraceae](species/Asteraceae.md)

## Background & Historical Significance
- **1957:** First documented report of invasion in East Nepal by M.L. Banerji (*Invasion of Eupatorium glandulosum in East Nepal*).
- Native to Mexico and Central America, it is one of Nepal's most aggressive invasive alien species, invading forest margins, roadsides, and abandoned lands across elevations from 200m to 3,200m.
