# Leguminosae (Fabaceae)

**वानस्पतिक परिवार: लेगुमिनोसी (फाबेसी)**

Leguminosae (also known as Fabaceae or the pea/legume family) is a large and economically important family of flowering plants. In Nepal, several invasive alien species belong to this family.

## प्रमुख बाह्य मिचाहा प्रजातिहरू (Invasive Species in Nepal)

- [Senna occidentalis (ठूलो टाप्रे / Coffee Senna)](<../26 species/Senna occidentalis.md>)
- [Senna tora (सानो टाप्रे / Sickle Senna)](<../26 species/Senna tora.md>)
